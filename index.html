Dayumay Clip Studio

A private tool for one creator.

Dayumay Clip Studio is a personal desktop tool that turns approved livestream highlights into short vertical videos (9:16) with readable captions, and publishes them to the creator's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts.

How it works

  1. The creator joins clipping campaigns in which streamers allow and pay for clips of their streams.
  2. The tool cuts the allowed source material into short vertical videos and adds captions and the required credit.
  3. YouTube: the finished Shorts are uploaded to the creator's own channel on a schedule through the YouTube Data API.
  4. TikTok: the creator opens the tool's posting screen, sees their account, reviews each video and caption, chooses the privacy and interaction settings, and confirms. Only then is the video sent through the TikTok Content Posting API.

The tool is used only by its owner. It does not offer accounts to other people and does not collect data from viewers.
