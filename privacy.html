Privacy Policy

Dayumay Clip Studio · Last updated: 19 October 2026

Dayumay Clip Studio ("the tool") is a personal desktop application used only by its owner to publish short videos to the owner's own social media accounts.

What data the tool uses

  • Account access tokens for TikTok, YouTube and Instagram, granted by the account owner through the official sign-in pages of these platforms.
  • Basic profile information (display name, username, avatar, allowed privacy options) needed to show the posting screen.
  • The videos and captions that the owner creates and chooses to publish.

How the data is stored

All tokens, videos and settings are stored only on the owner's own computer. The tool has no server and no database in the cloud. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Third parties

Videos are sent only to the platform the owner chooses (TikTok, YouTube or Instagram) through their official APIs. The use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting data and revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the security settings of the TikTok, Google or Instagram account. For Google/YouTube, access can be revoked on the Google security settings page. Deleting the tool's folder removes all locally stored data, including the stored access tokens.
